The problem

Most marketing gets paid whether it works or not.

If you've tried ads or hired an agency before, this probably sounds familiar.

✕Ads that burn money

Boosted posts and broad campaigns that get likes, not customers. Money goes out and you can't tell what came back.

✕Agencies paid no matter what

Monthly retainers and setup fees whether they deliver or not. They get paid either way, so the risk is all on you.

✕No real tracking

Reports full of impressions and clicks, but no clear answer to the only question that matters: how many sales did this bring in?

We flipped the model. If we don't bring you sales, we don't get paid.

How it works

Six steps from first call to new customers.

Simple terms: we only get paid when we perform.

01

Free strategy call

A quick 15-minute call to learn your business, your best customers and your goals, and to see if we're a fit. No pressure and no cost.

02

Audit of your current marketing

We look at your website, Google listing, social pages and any past ads to find what's leaking leads and where the quickest wins are.

03

We build the campaign and creatives

Offer, ad copy, images and video, audience targeting, and a landing page built to convert. You approve everything before it goes live.

04

Launch with full tracking

Campaigns go live on Facebook/Meta and Google with tracking set up end to end, so every result traces back to the ad that brought it in.

  • Call tracking for phone leads
  • Form and booking tracking
05

Weekly optimizing and reporting

We test new ads and audiences every week, cut what isn't working and scale what is. You get a clear weekly report of leads, sales and what we changed.

06

FAQ

Questions business owners ask us

Don't see yours? Ask us on a call.

How does "pay only on results" work?

We take a small % of the sales we generate through the ads. Nothing upfront and nothing else comes out of your pocket. If we don't perform, you drop us.

What % do you take?

It's agreed per business, based on your offer and goals. Ask us on a call and we'll walk you through it.

We set up the Meta pixel, Google conversion tracking, call tracking and form tracking so every lead and sale can be traced back to the ads we run.

What platforms do you advertise on?

Mainly Facebook/Meta (including Instagram) and Google. We pick based on where your customers are.

Who pays the ad spend?

We'll go over how ad spend is handled on the call so it fits your business.

Do I need a website?

It helps. If you don't have one or it's outdated, we build landing pages and full websites made for ads. Ask us on a call.

Is there a contract or lock-in?

No long-term lock-in. We perform or you drop us.

Once terms are agreed and access and tracking are set up, we start building and testing campaigns. We'll give you a timeline on the call.

Do I get to approve the ads?

Yes. You see and approve the creatives, copy and offer before anything goes live.

Do you only work in Barrie?

We're local guys from Barrie and love working with businesses across Simcoe County and the GTA, but we also work with online brands anywhere.

What do you need from me?

Access to your ad accounts (or we set them up), your best offer, and a quick way to tell us when leads turn into sales. We handle the rest.
